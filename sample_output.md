# Research brief: Analyze the food delivery market in Nepal

## Summary
The Nepalese online food delivery market is expanding driven by rising internet and smartphone use, urban lifestyle demand, and post‑COVID restaurant partnerships. Consumers increasingly seek plant‑based, healthy, and locally flavored meals, while mobile app innovations improve ordering experience. However, limited digital payment options and logistical challenges constrain growth.

## Competitors
- **Foodmandu**: Founded in 2010, it enjoys the highest brand recall among Nepalese consumers.
- **Bhoj**: Launched three years later, ranks second but faces delivery inefficiencies and intense competition.

## Key observations
- Demand for plant‑based, healthy, and traditional Nepali dishes is rising.
- Mobile app innovations enhance user experience and customization.
- Internet penetration, smartphone usage, and urban busy lifestyles are key growth drivers.
- Limited digital payment infrastructure and low credit‑card penetration hinder expansion.
- Last‑mile delivery logistics and overall infrastructure challenges affect service efficiency.
