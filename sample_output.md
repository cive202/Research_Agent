# Research brief: Analyze the food delivery market in Nepal

## Summary
The food delivery market in Nepal shows modest growth driven by smartphone adoption and pandemic‑induced demand, but the evidence is thin and lacks quantitative market‑size data. Foodmandu has over 500,000 registered users and secured Series B funding, while commission rates vary across platforms. Competition is intense and delivery inefficiencies remain a challenge.

## Competitors
- **Foodmandu**: has over 500,000 registered users and secured Series B funding with Team Ventures taking a 20% stake [\[F3 · play.google.com\]](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.app.foodmandu&hl=en_US)[\[F4 · teamventures.com.np\]](https://www.teamventures.com.np/news/business/foodtech-startup-foodmandu-raises-series-b-to-enter-new-cities).
- **Foodbusters**: charges restaurants a commission of about 14‑15% [\[F1 · recordnepal.com\]](https://www.recordnepal.com/the-business-of-delivering-food).

## Key observations
- Commission fees differ: Foodbusters 14‑15%, Foodmandu ~22%, Bhoj ~20%, Pathao Food ~18% [\[F1 · recordnepal.com\]](https://www.recordnepal.com/the-business-of-delivering-food).
- Foodmandu has over 500,000 registered users and supports QR, digital wallets, cards and cash on delivery [\[F3 · play.google.com\]](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.app.foodmandu&hl=en_US).
- The COVID‑19 pandemic accelerated food‑delivery activity in Kathmandu, prompting many restaurants to partner with platforms [\[F5 · e-research.siam.edu\]](https://e-research.siam.edu/wp-content/uploads/2021/12/BBA-2020-COOP-Analysis-of-Food-Delivery-during-COVID-19.pdf).
- Growth is driven by rising smartphone penetration and changing lifestyles, but delivery inefficiencies and intense competition are key challenges [\[F6 · statista.com\]](https://www.statista.com/outlook/emo/online-food-delivery/meal-delivery/restaurant-delivery/nepal)[\[F7 · scribd.com\]](https://www.scribd.com/document/1035942655/Ecommerce-Report).
- Foodmandu's market success has spurred entry of several other food‑delivery startups in Nepal [\[F8 · teamventures.com.np\]](https://www.teamventures.com.np/news/business/foodtech-startup-foodmandu-raises-series-b-to-enter-new-cities).

## Evidence (findings the brief may cite)
- **F1** Commission fees charged to restaurants differ by platform, with Foodbusters charging about 14‑15%, Foodmandu about 22%, Bhoj around 20% and Pathao Food roughly 18% ([The business of delivering food - The Record](https://www.recordnepal.com/the-business-of-delivering-food))
- **F2** Restaurants in Nepal usually partner with multiple delivery platforms to maximise order volume and tap into each platform’s loyal customer base ([The business of delivering food - The Record](https://www.recordnepal.com/the-business-of-delivering-food))
- **F3** Foodmandu reports having over 500,000 registered users and supports payment via QR codes, digital wallets, debit/credit cards and cash on delivery ([Foodmandu - Apps on Google Play](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.app.foodmandu&hl=en_US))
- **F4** Foodmandu secured a Series B funding round to fund expansion into new Nepali cities and into grocery, vegetable and beverage segments; the round involved Team Ventures acquiring a 20% stake, following earlier investment from True North Associates in 2016 ([Foodtech Startup Foodmandu Raises Series B To Enter New Cities - Team Ventures](https://www.teamventures.com.np/news/business/foodtech-startup-foodmandu-raises-series-b-to-enter-new-cities))
- **F5** The COVID‑19 pandemic accelerated the food‑delivery market in Nepal, especially in Kathmandu, as many restaurants began collaborating with delivery services to reach customers ([Analysis of Food Delivery during COVID-19 Pandemic at Foodmandu](https://e-research.siam.edu/wp-content/uploads/2021/12/BBA-2020-COOP-Analysis-of-Food-Delivery-during-COVID-19.pdf))
- **F6** Mild growth in Nepal’s restaurant‑delivery segment is being driven by rising smartphone penetration, changing consumer lifestyles and a growing preference for convenient meal options ([Restaurant Delivery - Nepal | Statista Market Forecast](https://www.statista.com/outlook/emo/online-food-delivery/meal-delivery/restaurant-delivery/nepal))
- **F7** Key challenges for Nepali food‑delivery services include delivery inefficiencies and intense competition among platforms ([Ecommerce Report | PDF | Restaurants | E Commerce](https://www.scribd.com/document/1035942655/Ecommerce-Report))
- **F8** Foodmandu’s market success has spurred the entry of several other food‑delivery startups in Nepal ([Foodtech Startup Foodmandu Raises Series B To Enter New Cities - Team Ventures](https://www.teamventures.com.np/news/business/foodtech-startup-foodmandu-raises-series-b-to-enter-new-cities))
