# Structure profile

How a brief is laid out. A guideline's "Constraints" section overrides anything here.

- **Summary**: 2-4 sentences. The first sentence answers the guideline's angle directly
  (or says the evidence cannot answer it). Name the market, the country and the year of the newest figure.
- **Competitors**: at most two. Pick the two the findings say the most about. One sentence each on
  why it matters, with a figure if the findings have one.
- **Key observations**: 3-5 bullets. One fact per bullet, specific enough to be checked:
  a name, a number, a date or a place. Order them from most to least important for the angle.
- **Sources**: only pages the findings came from, with title and URL.
- **Missing information**: what the guideline asked for that the evidence does not cover.
  Be specific ("no market size figure newer than 2021"), not generic ("more data needed").
- Total length: a reader should finish the brief in under two minutes (roughly 200-300 words).
