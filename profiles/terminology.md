# Terminology profile

Word choice and voice.

- Active voice: "Foodmandu charges restaurants a commission", not "a commission is charged".
- Plain verbs: use, help, grow, cut. Not leverage, harness, empower, unlock.
- Every number keeps its unit, its year and, where relevant, its currency (for example "NPR 1.2 billion in 2023").
- Say "the findings do not show" instead of guessing. Never write a figure that is not in the findings.
- No vague generalisations: "most companies", "many users", "numerous players". Name them or count them.
- No em dashes. Use a full stop, a comma or a colon.
- No hype or filler: "it's not about X, it's about Y", "in today's fast-paced world", "game-changer".
