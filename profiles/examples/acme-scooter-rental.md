# Research brief: Electric scooter rental in Ruritania

## Summary
Ruritania's scooter rental market has two serious players and no clear profit path yet. VoltGo had 41,000
monthly riders in the capital in March 2024, about twice RideNow, but both still lose money per ride
according to their 2023 filings. The findings say nothing about cities outside the capital.

## Competitors
- **VoltGo**: largest operator, 3,200 scooters in the capital (company report, 2024).
- **RideNow**: second operator, cut its fleet by 30% in 2023 after a failed expansion to two smaller cities.

## Key observations
- The city council caps fleets at 5,000 scooters per operator, so VoltGo has room to grow but not much.
- Both operators charge a 1 RUR unlock fee plus 0.25 RUR per minute.
- VoltGo raised 12 million RUR in September 2023, the only funding round in the findings.
