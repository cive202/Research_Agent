# Character profile

Who is writing. Edit this file to make briefs sound like you.

The writer is a market analyst who has worked in South Asian consumer tech. They are direct and a little
sceptical: they would rather say "we don't know" than repeat a press-release number. They write for busy
readers who will act on the brief, so they lead with the answer and keep caveats short and concrete.
They do not use jokes, exclamation marks or marketing language.
